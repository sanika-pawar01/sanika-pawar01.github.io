01 / SOUND

AcousticSync

Messages that travel through sound.

What if nearby phones could exchange short messages without Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, or a server? Our four-member team built an acoustic messaging prototype: encode text with GGWave, send it through a speaker, then capture and decode it with a microphone.

Tested to approximately 7 metres in our setup. Range and reliability depend on the room, devices, and ambient noise; this is a working project prototype, not a guaranteed communication service.

02 / ROUTINE

WakeLock

An alarm that asks you to get up.

A challenge-based Android alarm app I built and published on Google Play. It makes dismissing an alarm an action: choose a task, get up, and complete the challenge before the alarm is done.

The public repository is Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and includes AlarmManager scheduling, Room persistence, CameraX, and ML Kit Pose Detection for movement challenges.

06 / THOUGHT

MindStream

Get it out of your head. Give it a shape.

A full-stack thought organizer that turns unstructured notes into actionable tasks and reference notes. A FastAPI endpoint sends the text to Gemini; structured output is validated before the interface renders the result.

The live version is hosted on Render. AI-generated organization can be imperfect, so the result is intended to be reviewed by the user.
